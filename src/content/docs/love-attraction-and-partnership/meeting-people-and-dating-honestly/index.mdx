<div class="ds-head__lede">💡 <strong>An honest gap.</strong> Every book in this cluster assumes a Western dating market — apps, serial dating, individual choice. Kathmandu, family expectation, and the reality that arranged and semi-arranged introductions are still normal in Nepal change the mechanics substantially. <strong>The psychology transfers; the logistics don't.</strong> No book on this list solves that, and I shouldn't pretend one does. What transfers is: knowing my attachment pattern, knowing that &quot;perfect&quot; is a trap, and knowing what predicts a lasting partnership. How I meet someone is a local question, and family conversations are part of it.</div>

### What has been tested

Not an empirical programme. The attention-residue result it leans on is Leroy 2009, one study, small, and narrower than the book's use of it. The 10,000-hour framing it borrows from Ericsson is contested by Macnamara's 2014 meta-analysis, which put deliberate practice at ~12% of variance across domains.

### What critics say

Read as absolutist about email and open offices. Practitioners in on-call, support and client-facing roles report the scheduling advice fails outright. Several reviewers note the exemplars are overwhelmingly tenured academics and solo authors — people with structural control over their calendars that most readers do not have.

### What has aged badly

The 2016 social-media chapter predates short-form video, which is what most people now actually lose the hours to. The open-plan critique also predates the distributed-work shift, which changed the interruption channel from the desk to the notification.
